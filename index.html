PRO-FUNCHEESE

Functional, high-protein, gluten-free mini-cheesecakes, structured with micellar casein.

We develop and validate a portioned dessert with no gluten and no added sugar: an almond-flour base, a quark and raspberry cream, and a protein gelling system that replaces bovine gelatin with micellar casein. The aim is a firmer, cleaner-cutting cream that also qualifies as "high protein", ready for production by the beneficiary.

About the project

A gluten-free dessert that does real nutritional work

SAVEUR launched its first gluten-free product through a 2017 CEC voucher developed with USAMV Cluj-Napoca. Consumer feedback confirmed the demand. This project extends the range beyond classic pastry, toward desserts with higher nutritional value.

Mini-cheesecakes are a hard matrix to reformulate. Each portion combines a baked base and a set cream, and the cream must hold its shape, cut cleanly and stay stable in cold storage. Removing gluten and added sugar takes away two structural tools. Raising protein to "high protein" levels adds a third constraint.

The central innovation is the protein gelling system of the cream. A bovine gelatin control is compared with micellar casein, a native milk protein with a complete essential amino acid profile, high water binding and slow digestion. The comparison is technological (rheology, texture, stability) and nutritional (composition, amino acids, in vitro digestibility).

Core comparison · WP2

Two ways to set a cream

Gelatin sets by forming a thermoreversible network of helical junction zones. Micellar casein builds a dense, creamy structure through colloidal particles that bind water, without classical thermal gelation.

Control · bovine gelatin

Collagen-derived network

Partial hydrolysis product of collagen. Gels at 1-3%, sets at 20-30 °C and melts near 30-35 °C, giving an elastic texture and a clean cut. Its amino acid profile is dominated by glycine (≈33%) and proline + hydroxyproline (22-25%), with very little essential amino acid content.

Innovation · micellar casein

Native casein micelles

About 80% of milk protein, isolated by micro- or ultrafiltration that keeps the native micelles (100-300 nm) stabilised by colloidal calcium phosphate. Binds 3-4 g water per g protein, structures creams at 3-6%, and is digested slowly over 5-7 h, with leucine at 8-10%.

Regulatory target · WP3

When is a dessert "high protein"?

Under Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006, a food may claim "high protein" when at least 20% of its energy value comes from protein, and "source of protein" from 12%. Energy is calculated with the conversion factors of Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011. Try the calculation below.

Main technological risk

Protein falls short of the "high protein" threshold

The matrix must combine adequate texture, structural stability and sensory acceptance without gluten or added sugar, which limits how much protein can be added.

Mitigation: controlled addition of whey protein isolate, adjusted until the threshold is reached.

Commercial risk · low

Market dynamics and purchasing power

Consumer behaviour depends on the wider economy. Demand for gluten-free, no-added-sugar and high-protein desserts is growing.

Mitigation: established brand, a proven gluten-free launch in 2017, and a product validated technologically and nutritionally before launch.
